# HR-Analytics-Dashboard

## Project Overview

This HR Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI solution designed to help organisations monitor workforce performance, analyse employee attrition, and support data-driven HR decision-making. The dashboard transforms raw employee data into actionable insights, enabling stakeholders to identify retention challenges, workforce trends, and key areas for improvement.

## Objectives

* Monitor overall workforce metrics.
* Analyse employee attrition across multiple dimensions.
* Identify departments, job roles, and employee segments with high turnover.
* Support HR teams in developing effective retention strategies.
* Enable interactive filtering and drill-down analysis.

## Dashboard Features

### Employee Overview

Provides a high-level summary of workforce statistics, including total employees, active employees, attrition count, attrition rate, average income, and average experience.

### Attrition Analysis by Education

Visualises employee attrition based on educational background, helping identify which education groups contribute most to employee turnover.

### Attrition Analysis by Salary Slab

Highlights the relationship between salary ranges and attrition rates, allowing HR teams to assess compensation-related retention risks.

### Attrition by Job Role & Job Satisfaction

Displays attrition trends across different job roles and satisfaction levels, helping identify high-risk positions and employee engagement concerns.

### Age Group Distribution

Shows workforce demographics by age category, enabling organisations to understand employee composition and succession planning needs.

### Attrition Trend by Experience

Analyses employee exits based on years of experience to uncover retention patterns throughout the employee lifecycle.

### Department-wise Attrition

Compares attrition across departments to identify areas requiring immediate HR intervention.

### Interactive Filters

Users can dynamically filter dashboard insights by:

* Department
* Gender

## Business Insights

* Research & Development experiences the highest employee attrition.
* Lower salary bands show significantly higher turnover rates.
* Certain job roles contribute disproportionately to overall attrition.
* Employees with lower job satisfaction are more likely to leave the organisation.
* Early-career employees demonstrate higher attrition tendencies.

## Recommendations

1. Improve retention initiatives within high-attrition departments.
2. Review compensation structures for lower salary bands.
3. Develop career growth and mentorship programs.
4. Enhance employee engagement and satisfaction monitoring.
5. Implement predictive HR analytics for proactive retention planning.
